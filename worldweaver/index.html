TWITCH INTEGRATION

Bring your real chat in.

Use Twitch EventSub to turn your viewers' chat messages into live world actions. No bot password, no secret pasted into this site.

01 Create a Twitch developer app (OAuth Redirect URL must match the URL below).
02 Paste your public Client ID, then authorize Twitch with the minimal user:read:chat scope.
03 Post !seed, !rain, !build, or !spark in your own channel.

OAuth token stays only in your browser session. We never ask for your Twitch password. To disconnect, revoke the token in Twitch settings.
THE EXPERIENCE

Every message leaves a mark.

Worldweaver transforms a Twitch stream's chat into a cooperative living ecosystem. It is a shared creation, not a winner-takes-all competition.

1. Chat shapes the world. Four simple commands create trees, rivers, villages and fireflies live on screen.

2. Collaboration unlocks magic. Distinct viewers activating all four forces earn a Harmony Combo and extra community energy.

3. Reach the shared quest. When everyone completes a chapter's four goals, the host moves the world into the next chapter.

4. Keep your world. Export the replay as JSON and celebrate every builder who took part.
